What the app does

All Nigerian news in one place

Fresh headlines from national, business, sports, technology and online publishers, updated throughout the day.

One story, many perspectives

When several publishers report the same event, the app groups their reports so you can compare coverage.

Always the original source

Every story shows its publisher, and the full article opens on the publisher's own website.

Save, search and go offline

Bookmark stories, search recent headlines and read saved headlines without a connection.

How we source news

All Nigerian Newspapers is a news aggregator developed by Emerging Technologies. Headlines and short previews come from each publisher's public news feed and are always shown with the publisher's name and logo. The app does not write or edit news, and it does not republish full articles: tapping a story opens the original article on the publisher's website. Headlines, articles, images, logos and trademarks belong to their respective publishers.

Publishers who would like a correction, a change to how their content appears, or removal can contact us.
