Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

All Nigerian Newspapers ("the app") is a free news reader. This policy explains what information is used when you use it.

No account, no personal profile

The app has no sign-up or login. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number, and we do not build a personal profile on our servers.

Information stored on your phone

  • Your preferences (interests, preferred papers, dark mode, Data Saver, notification choices).
  • Saved stories and saved papers.
  • Cached headlines, so the app works offline.
  • Basic, anonymous advertising statistics (for example how many ads loaded), used to keep ads working reliably.

This information stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

News feeds

To show headlines, your phone downloads public news feeds and images directly from each publisher's website. Those publishers receive the normal technical information any website receives (such as your IP address). Their own privacy policies apply when you open an article on their site.

Notifications

If you turn on alerts, the app uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) to deliver them. Your device receives a messaging token and subscribes to topics such as "breaking news" or a category you chose. We do not link this token to your identity. You can turn alerts off at any time in Settings › Notifications.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address and interaction data to serve, measure and personalise ads, subject to your choices. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent using Google's consent tools, and you can change your choice at any time using "Manage ad privacy choices" on the app's Privacy Policy screen. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Children

The app is a general news service and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app changes; the date above shows the latest version.
