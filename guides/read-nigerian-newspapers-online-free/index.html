How to Read Nigerian Newspapers Online for Free

Every free way to read Nigerian newspapers online in 2026: publisher websites, e-papers, and one app that puts 100 newspapers on your phone.

Nigerian newspapers publish most of their reporting online for free. The challenge is not access, it is time: Punch, Vanguard, Daily Trust, The Sun, ThisDay and Premium Times all live on different websites. Here are the free options, and the quickest one.

1. Newspaper websites (free)

Almost every Nigerian newspaper publishes its stories free on its website. Bookmark the ones you read most:

  • The Punch: punchng.com
  • Vanguard: vanguardngr.com
  • The Sun: thesun.ng
  • Daily Trust: dailytrust.com
  • THISDAY: thisdaylive.com
  • Premium Times: premiumtimesng.com
  • Sahara Reporters: saharareporters.com
  • Channels TV: channelstv.com
  • Tribune: tribuneonlineng.com
  • Leadership: leadership.ng
  • Daily Post: dailypost.ng
  • Legit.ng: legit.ng

For the full list, see all 100 Nigerian newspapers and their websites.

2. E-papers (usually paid)

Some publishers sell digital replicas of the printed paper. They are useful if you want the exact print layout, but most need a subscription per newspaper.

3. All newspapers in one free app

All Nigerian Newspapers brings headlines from 100 Nigerian newspapers into one feed on Android. It is free, needs no account, and every story opens on the original publisher's website, so you are still reading the newspaper itself.

  • Save time: one feed instead of a dozen websites.
  • Compare: stories reported by several papers are grouped.
  • Save data: Data Saver skips list images; headlines load as light text.
  • Read later: bookmark stories and browse loaded headlines offline.

Tips for reading Nigerian news online

  • Check more than one source for big stories; coverage differs between papers.
  • Look at the publish time; breaking stories are often updated.
  • Prefer the original publisher's page over reposted copies.
