What you get

Permissions, explained

The app uses the internet to load publisher feeds and notifications only if you turn alerts on. It does not ask for your contacts, location, camera or files. Read the privacy policy.

Download questions

Is the download free?

Yes. All Nigerian Newspapers is free on Google Play. There are no subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. The app is currently available for Android phones on Google Play.

How much data does it use?

Headlines are text and very light. Turn on Data Saver in Settings to skip list images and reduce background refreshes.

Why does the app ask for notification permission?

Only if you turn on news alerts (breaking news and the daily digest). You can turn them off anytime in Settings › Notifications.
