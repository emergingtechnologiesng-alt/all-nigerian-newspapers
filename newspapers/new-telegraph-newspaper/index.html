New Telegraph Newspaper FAQ

How can I read New Telegraph Newspaper headlines today?

Install the free All Nigerian Newspapers app. New Telegraph appears in your feed with today's headlines alongside other Nigerian newspapers. Tap any headline, then Read on New Telegraph to open the full article on newtelegraphng.com.

Is this the official New Telegraph Newspaper app?

No. All Nigerian Newspapers is an independent news aggregator. It shows New Telegraph headlines with the publisher's name and links every story to the original article on newtelegraphng.com. New Telegraph's content and trademarks belong to its publisher.

Is reading New Telegraph in the app free?

Yes. The app is free and needs no account. Articles open on New Telegraph's own website, where the publisher's usual access applies.

What does New Telegraph cover?

In the app, New Telegraph headlines appear under Politics, Business, Sports, Entertainment, Technology, Health, Education, Lifestyle.
